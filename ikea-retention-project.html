Power BI DAX Customer retention

Project overview

This Power BI project brings customer retention measures into a dashboard, making it possible to explore churn, repeat purchasing, loyalty, and customer lifetime value across different regions and store types.

What the dashboard explores

  • Customer churn and repeat purchase behavior
  • Loyalty points and customer lifetime value
  • Regional and store-type comparisons
  • Customer segments, including views by income
